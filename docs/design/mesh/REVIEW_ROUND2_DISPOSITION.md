**Key.**
- **A**: accepted; the text or the design changed, and the cell says where.
- **OD**: the text changed, but closing the finding needs an owner ruling; the cell names the decision.
- **R**: rejected, with the reason.

Where a reviewer offered alternatives, the cell names the one taken. Where an owner acting decision (`OWNER_DIRECTIVES_2026-09-30.md`, AD-1…AD-6) superseded the reviewer's remedy, the cell says so.

- **By severity:** 8 high, 15 medium, 12 low.
- **Nothing was rejected.** Every finding identified a real defect in r2.
- **Two remedies were superseded, not rejected.** R2-CONFORMANCE-1 and R2-DIRECTIVES-1 proposed strict roadmap order (v1.1, v2, v2.5, v3, then v4) as the default plan. After the review, the owner delegated the sequencing decision, and AD-1 decided a reordered ladder. r3 therefore takes the reviewers' *diagnosis*: the plan must not claim to keep an order it changes.
